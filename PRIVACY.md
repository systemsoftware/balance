# Privacy Policy for Balance Browser

**Last Updated:** July 9, 2026

Balance Browser ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Balance Browser handles your data. Balance Browser is designed with a privacy-first approach, meaning the vast majority of your data stays on your device.

## 1. Data We Collect and Store Locally

As a web browser, Balance Browser stores certain information locally on your device to function correctly and improve your experience. This data is **not** transmitted to our servers.

*   **Browsing History:** Websites you visit, if history recording is enabled. You can disable history recording in settings.
*   **Bookmarks and Favorites:** Websites you pin or save.
*   **Passwords:** Passwords you choose to save in the built-in Password Manager are securely stored on your device (e.g., using macOS Keychain).
*   **Notes:** Any text entered into the built-in Notepad.
*   **Settings and Preferences:** Your customizations, such as Homepage, User Agent, Sidebar configuration, and browser profiles.
*   **Cookies and Local Storage:** Data stored by websites you visit, governed by the privacy policies of those respective websites.

## 2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features

Balance Browser includes integrated AI features, such as Local AI Chat and AI Page Summarization. 

*   **On-Device Processing:** All AI models (e.g., Apple Foundation Models) run **locally on your device**.
*   **No Cloud Transmission:** Your chat conversations, page content for summarization, and data used for event or entity extraction are **not** sent to any external servers or third-party AI providers. 

## 3. Email Integration (IMAP)

If you configure an IMAP email account within a browser profile:
*   Your email credentials are encrypted and stored locally on your device.
*   The browser connects directly to your email provider's servers to fetch unread emails. We do not intermediate this connection or have access to your emails or credentials.

## 4. Extensions

Balance Browser supports Chrome Web Store extensions.
*   When you install and use third-party extensions, they may collect their own data as described in their respective privacy policies.
*   We do not control the data collection practices of third-party extensions. Please review the privacy policy of any extension before installing it.

## 5. Third-Party Websites

When you use Balance Browser to navigate the internet, you are visiting third-party websites.
*   This Privacy Policy does not apply to the practices of websites you visit.
*   Those websites may collect data, use cookies, and track your activity according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the sites you visit.
*   Balance Browser provides features like Content Blocking to help you manage your privacy on third-party sites.

## 6. Data Sharing

Since we do not collect your personal data or browsing activity on our servers, **we do not share, sell, or rent your data to any third parties.**

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and by updating the file in our repository.

## 8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please refer to the project repository's issue tracker.
